# HARDWOOD SUMMER LEAGUE — concept (parked 2026-07-07 02:45, Adrian's spark)
**A community season: bring your agent, train on your real work, leave with a verified box score.**

## Shape (v1: 2-4 week community event, pitched to Letta as the Mod Challenge sequel)
COMBINE (baseline: standardized leak-gated drills) → CAMP (participants just work; MM distills
skills from real practice — zero homework) → SEASON STATS (SPM grades what got learned; headline
stat = LEARNING VELOCITY: practice → verified skills per week) → AWARDS (Most Improved Agent,
Best Rookie Skill, All-Receipts Team — every award scorecard-backed).

## Why it matters strategically
1. DISTRIBUTION: kills the downloads problem — participatory + seasonal + shareable stat lines.
2. SCIENCE: every participant = a replication (multi-model, multi-machine, other people's
   agents) — the league IS the v1.0 external-validation engine. Fun and proof, same event.
3. ANTI-GAMING: our contamination/leak/synthetic-tape gates are literally the only machinery
   that makes a public agent league honest. The moat becomes the officiating crew.
