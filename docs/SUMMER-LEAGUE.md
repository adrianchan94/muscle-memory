## One line
**A community season: bring your agent, train it on your real work, leave with a verified box
score.** The first agent development program where the stats are real — officiated by the only
crew with anti-gaming machinery already built.

## Why (the three birds)
1. **Distribution** — kills the downloads problem. A league is participatory, seasonal, and
   shareable: nobody posts a benchmark paper; everybody posts their rookie's stat line.
2. **Science** — every participant is a replication: multi-model, multi-machine, other
   people's agents, real work. The league IS the v1.0 external-validation engine. The
   community has fun; the methodology gets its n. Same event.
3. **Category** — whoever runs the league sets the standard. The officiating crew becomes the
   institution.

## The season (v1 = 3 weeks, capped ~50 agents, Letta community)

### Week 0 — THE COMBINE (baseline, one evening)
- Participant installs muscle-memory (`letta install npm:@letta-ai/muscle-memory`) + registers
  for the league (a manifest: agent handle, model, opt-in stat visibility).
- Baseline measurement: a standardized, sealed, leak-gated drill set (authored fresh each
  season by the officiating crew, never reused — holdout discipline) establishes each agent's
  no-skill floor. Zero configuration: a league runner script does everything.

### Weeks 1-3 — TRAINING CAMP (the beautiful part: there is no homework)
- Participants just... do their normal work. MM watches real sessions, distills, stages.
- Weekly "practice reports" auto-generated: candidates observed, skills staged/graduated,
  usage minutes. Participants review/approve their staged skills (their agents, their calls).
- Optional weekly SCRIMMAGES: opt-in community drill nights (shared task, one hour, box
  scores published same night) — the social heartbeat, streamed/office-hours friendly.

### Week 4 — THE MEASUREMENT (the whistle)
- Each agent's shelf faces its season exam: per-skill SPM verdicts on drills scaffolded from
  the skill's own domain (FRONTIER #3 scaffolder) + the sealed common set for cross-agent
  comparability. OFF-arm pilots gate every drill — the league never grades guessables.
- Every verdict ships with n, CI, and claim label. "Insufficient" is a printable, honorable
  result — the league teaches the culture by scoring with it.

## THE STATS (what makes it a sport)
| Stat | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **LV — Learning Velocity** | verified skills earned per week of real practice (the headline) |
| **SPM** | per-skill plus-minus on the season exam |
| **NET** | shelf net rating: Σ per-skill verdicts weighted by usage minutes |
| **DISC** | discipline rating: staged-review hygiene, zero contamination flags |
| **MIN** | real usage minutes (retrievals that mattered) |

## THE AWARDS (all scorecard-backed, no vibes trophies)
🏆 Most Improved Agent (Δ floor→season NET) · 🥇 Best Rookie Skill (highest single-skill SPM
with n≥ threshold) · 🛡 All-Receipts Team (perfect DISC) · 🎯 Sixth Man (best niche skill —
narrow context, huge plus-minus) · 🚫 The Honest Null (best-documented "this skill did
nothing" finding — yes, we award the null; that's the whole culture in one trophy).

## OFFICIATING & INTEGRITY (our moat IS the league's credibility)
- Sealed seasonal drill sets (fresh authorship, holdout rotation — ULTRON's lane).
- Leak gates on every drill; synthetic-tape gates on every tape; provenance required per row.
- Anti-sandbagging: combine baselines are OFF-arm, mechanically scored.
- Privacy: skills stay private by default; only STATS publish (opt-in per stat). Scorecards
  sanitization-scanned before any share (machinery built, v0.9).
- Appeals = receipts: every number links to its run artifacts.
