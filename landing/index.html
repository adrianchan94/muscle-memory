THE PROBLEM

Your agent solved this yesterday.
It will solve it again tomorrow. From scratch.

Every session, the world's smartest models re-learn your codebase's landmines, re-discover your conventions, re-make the same fix — and remember none of it. The industry's answer is bigger models. But the next model still won't know the things only your work can teach.
The gap is structural. Models know everything public and nothing local. Your conventions, your pitfalls, your hard-won fixes — that knowledge lives nowhere.
And the tools that try to capture it have a garbage problem: skill libraries that grow like weeds, with no way to know what's actually worth keeping.
HARDWOOD exists to fix both. →

THE RECEIPTS

Our benchmark blocked itself.
Three times.

Anyone can show you their wins. We keep the fouls on the tape — because an instrument you can't watch fail is an instrument you can't trust.

BLOCKED · EXIT 18
Judge transport dead — 42/42 calls failed
Rather than score an eval with no judge, the run self-labeled BLOCKED and committed the failure. The receipt ships in the repo.
CULLED
Leak gate — 5 of 6 "impossible" drills were guessable
The OFF-arm pilot proved our own drill design leaked. The gate now runs before every registration — the corpus can't lie to us anymore.
CAPPED
"Directional" at p = 0.0002
Our best-ever result stayed capped because it was five pairs short of the pre-registered n. The rules don't bend for good news either.
