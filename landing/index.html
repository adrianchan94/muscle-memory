Skill Plus-Minus · the officiating crew

Every skill gets a box score.

The finding that matters: skill value is signed. Knowledge the model lacks is worth +0.64. Knowledge it already has is worth nothing — sometimes less. No system that measures by vibes can see the difference. Ours prints it.

The whistle works both ways

Our benchmark blocked itself. Three times.

The instrument refuses to produce numbers it can't stand behind — and every refusal is a committed receipt, not a deleted log.

BLOCKED · exit 18 — judge transport dead (42/42 calls failed)
Rather than score an eval with no judge, the run self-labeled BLOCKED and committed the failure. The receipt ships in the repo.
CULLED · leak gate — 5 of 6 "impossible" drills were guessable
The OFF-arm pilot proved our own drill design leaked. The gate now runs before every registration — the corpus can't lie to us anymore.
CAPPED · "directional" at p = 0.0002
Our best-ever result stayed capped at "directional" because it was 5 pairs short of the pre-registered n. The rules don't bend for good news either.
