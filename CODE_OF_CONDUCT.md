# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Muscle Memory project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Assuming good faith while still demanding receipts
- Giving and accepting constructive adversarial review
- Focusing on evidence, not ego
- Being careful with security-sensitive reports

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks
- Publishing others’ private information without consent
- Social-engineering maintainers for publish/push access
- Intentionally introducing misleading claims or fabricated receipts

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior via a private GitHub security advisory or maintainer contact on `@adrianchan94`.

Maintainers may remove commits, issues, or PRs that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for severe or repeated violations.
